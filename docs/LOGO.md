# Boplborne: logo and icon ideas

Brainstorm, 2026-10-21. Nothing here is drawn yet; pick one and it becomes a PixelLab job plus a hand pass (see `docs/ART_PIPELINE.md`).

## What the logo has to say

A co-op boss rush that is slapstick, not grim: a 1930s rubber-hose cartoon look, a theatre stage, a tower, a bell, and a blue blob knight with a plume. Players bop each other (friendly fire is on). The name reads "bop" + "-borne".

## Wordmark (the title)

The title sign already is a plum marquee with chasing bulbs and cream display lettering with an ink outline. Keep that as the wordmark and change only what an icon needs:

- Make the **O** in BOPLBORNE a bell, or a round blob face with two eyes (the mascot peeking through the letter).
- Lean the whole word a few degrees up to the right, like a hit that has just landed ("bop").
- Optional impact stars (the cream and orange ones from the stage) on the top-right corner.

## Icon concepts (square, readable at 16 px)

1. **The Bopped Bell (recommended).** A brass bell with a dent where it got bopped, a small star burst on the dent, the blue plume sticking out of the top. Tower and story in one picture (the silenced Grace Bell), and the silhouette stays clear when tiny.
2. **Blob in the spotlight.** The blue blob knight head-on, plume up, in a round spotlight on a plum background. Friendly and the most "game mascot", but says nothing about the tower.
3. **Marquee B.** A single big cream "B" ringed by marquee bulbs on plum. Matches the main menu exactly and is easy to draw cleanly; least unique.
4. **Fist and bell.** A white cartoon glove punching a bell, with a "BOP!" burst. Most slapstick; busier at small sizes.
5. **Tower top.** A small tower with the bell in its window and a crown of bulbs. Good for a store banner; too fine for an icon.

## Palette

Reuse the game's: plum `#7a3b5c` family for the background, cream for letters, ink for outlines, orange `#ff9a3c` for the accent, the P1 blue for the blob.
